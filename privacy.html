Matcha Tiles — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Matcha Tiles is a family tile-matching game made by Kady Chen ("we", "the developer"). This policy explains what the app stores, why, and your choices. The short version: no ads, no trackers, no data sales, no chat, and no real names needed — ever.

What the app stores

On your device

  • Players you create (display name, avatar, generation)
  • Game progress: trophies, streaks, leaves (the in-game leaf currency), shop items, settings
  • Friend nicknames — these are stored only on your device and are never uploaded

On our server (only if you play the connected features)

  • An anonymous account ID — a random identifier, created automatically. We do not ask for your name, email address, or phone number.
  • Optional: if you choose Sign in with Apple ("Save my game"), your game is linked to that Apple sign-in so it can be restored on a new device. We receive an anonymous Apple identifier, not your email, unless you separately choose to share it with Apple.
  • Player profiles for leaderboards and races: display name, avatar, generation, trophies, and race results.
  • Place names — city, state, and country only, if you add a place to your player. The app never stores GPS coordinates or precise location. You can type a place instead of using location permission, and you can play fully without any place at all.
  • Friends: friend codes and the friend connections you make with them. There is no chat or messaging in the app, and no way to send free text to other players.
  • Pack votes (which tile art style you voted for).
  • Drawings a player chooses to submit for the Family Art / community themes. Every drawing is reviewed by the developer before it can appear in the game. Submitting a drawing is always optional.

What we do NOT collect

  • No advertising identifiers, no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No contacts, photos (except a drawing you choose to submit), health, or financial data
  • No precise location, ever
  • We never sell personal data

Children

Matcha Tiles is designed to be safe for children: accounts are anonymous, there is no chat, no external links from gameplay, no advertising, and no in-app purchases offered to children. Drawings are moderated by the developer before appearing. Parents can delete all of a player's data (below).

Your choices and deletion

  • You can remove a player, clear a place name, and delete the app at any time; local data goes with it.
  • Delete my data: in the app, Players → My game → Delete my data erases this device's data and the account's cloud backup, and disconnects the account.
  • You can also email us and we will delete the account's remaining server records (profile, leaderboard entries, friends, votes) for you.

Retention

Server records are kept while the account is active so leaderboards, friends, and backups work. Deleted accounts are removed from our database; routine backups age out within 30 days.
